HOW TO ADD A REVIEW
1. On Goodreads, open your review and click "Blog This Review".
2. Copy the whole box, paste it below a "---" line.
3. (Optional) On a line by itself, add any of:
     @date Jan 2025  @spice 3  @tags omegaverse, reverse harem  @photo images/blog-photos/yourfile.jpg
   (any order, leave out what you don't want. @photo is YOUR OWN photo of the
   book — drop it in images/blog-photos/ first. No @photo? The card just
   uses the cover from your Goodreads snippet instead.)
4. End every review with a line that is just:  ---
5. Save and push. Reviews sort newest-first by the @date you give them.
Everything above the first "---" is ignored, so this note is safe to leave here.
To update the reading-challenge bar, edit the line just below (books read of your goal).
