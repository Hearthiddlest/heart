Drop your own book photos in this folder.

Then, in reviews.txt, add @photo to that review's optional line, e.g.:

  @date Jul 18, 2026  @spice 3  @photo images/blog-photos/marry-me-not.jpg

Any image works (jpg, png, heic-exported-as-jpg). If you skip @photo,
the card just falls back to the cover Goodreads gave in your snippet —
so nothing breaks if you haven't taken a photo of a book yet.

Tip: photos taken in portrait, reasonably close on the cover, look best —
the card crops to a tall rectangle so the title stays readable.
